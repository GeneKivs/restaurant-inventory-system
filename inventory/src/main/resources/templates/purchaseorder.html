Help - Purchase Order

This form allows you to create a new purchase order for inventory items. Follow these instructions carefully:

  • Item: Select the item you want to order from the dropdown list.
  • Supplier Name: Choose the supplier providing the item.
  • Date of Order: The current date is automatically filled in and cannot be edited.
  • Quantity: Enter the quantity of items you wish to order.
  • Unit: Displays the unit of measurement for the selected item and cannot be edited.
  • Save Order Button: Click this button to save the purchase order.

Notes:

  • Ensure that you have selected the correct item and supplier before submitting the order.
  • If you wish to exit without saving, click the EXIT button.
  • After saving, you can choose to print the purchase order.
