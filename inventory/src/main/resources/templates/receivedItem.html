Help - Received Item

This form allows you to record received items into the inventory system. Please follow the steps below:

  • Item: Select the item you are receiving from the dropdown list.
  • Order: Choose the corresponding purchase order for the item.
  • Received Quantity: Enter the quantity of the item that has been received.
  • Damaged Quantity: Enter any damaged quantity, if applicable (default is 0).
  • Unit: The unit of measurement for the selected item will be displayed, and cannot be edited.
  • Received Date: The current date is automatically filled in and cannot be edited.
  • Expiry Date: Enter the expiry date of the received item, if applicable.
  • Record Received Items Button: Click this button to save the received item record.

Notes:

  • Ensure that the correct item and purchase order are selected before submitting.
  • If you wish to exit without saving, click the EXIT button.
  • If the quantity received is different from what was ordered, make sure to enter the appropriate value in the "Received Quantity" field.
